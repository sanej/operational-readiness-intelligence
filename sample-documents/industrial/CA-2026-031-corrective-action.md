## 1. Origin

Raised against inspection finding **F-2026-014-01** (IR-2026-014, 2026-02-11):
non-drive-end dry gas seal secondary vent flow elevated to 21 Nm³/h against an OEM
alarm threshold of 15 Nm³/h, with a rising trend over eight months.

## 2. Immediate Action Taken

- Vent flow added to the daily operator round sheet with a hold point at 25 Nm³/h.
- Seal gas filter elements replaced 2026-02-18. No improvement in vent flow observed.
- OEM contacted for cartridge lead time. Confirmed 16 weeks from order.

## 3. Planned Corrective Action

1. Procure replacement dry gas seal cartridge (OEM part 44-DGS-NDE-C101).
2. Replace the non-drive-end seal cartridge during a machine outage.
3. Perform a static seal test and record baseline vent flow before return to service.
4. Update the seal condition baseline in the reliability database.

## 5. Risk Statement

Continued operation with a degrading primary seal face carries a risk of seal
failure in service, which would result in an unplanned trip and potential process
gas release to the secondary vent. The condition is being monitored but is not
being arrested.
