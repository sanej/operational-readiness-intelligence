## 2. Dry Gas Seal Cartridge Replacement

### 2.1 Prerequisites

Before this task may commence:

1. Energy isolation must be applied and verified in accordance with **SP-204**.
2. A General Work Permit must be in force.
3. The machine must be at ambient temperature — allow a minimum of 8 hours after
   shutdown before breaking the seal housing.
4. Replacement cartridge must be verified as the correct part number and within
   its shelf-life. **Cartridges exceeding 24 months in storage require OEM
   re-certification before fitting.**
5. A clean work area with controlled access must be established. Seal faces are
   damaged by particulate ingress.

### 2.2 Competency

Dry gas seal replacement may only be performed by a technician holding current
OEM seal-handling certification. Site competency records must be checked before
work is allocated.

### 2.3 Post-Replacement Requirements

1. Perform a static seal test at 5 barg nitrogen and hold for 30 minutes. Leakage
   must not exceed 2 Nm³/h.
2. Record the baseline vent flow after 4 hours of steady-state operation.
3. Update the seal condition baseline in the reliability database.

## 3. Major Overhaul — Internal Inspection

The 8-year major overhaul requires full disassembly, rotor removal, and internal
casing inspection. Last performed 2018-09. **Next due 2026-09.**

Overhaul scope includes:

- Rotor removal and runout check
- Journal and thrust bearing replacement
- Internal casing inspection for erosion and fouling
- Diaphragm and labyrinth seal clearance measurement
- Recoat of internal surfaces where specified

Overhaul requires a dedicated 14-day outage and a lifting plan for rotor removal.
