# SP-204 Energy Isolation and Lock-Out/Tag-Out

**Revision:** Rev 6 | **Effective:** 2025-09-15
**Applies to:** Rotating equipment in Gas Compression, including C-101

> Synthetic document created for demonstration purposes. Fictional site and asset.

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the minimum energy isolation requirements that must be
satisfied before any intrusive maintenance work begins on rotating equipment in
the Gas Compression system.

## 2. Isolation Requirements for Compressor C-101

Before any intrusive work, **all** of the following isolations must be applied,
verified, and locked:

### 2.1 Electrical Isolation

1. Rack out the driver breaker at MCC-4B and apply a personal lock and danger tag.
2. Isolate the lube oil pump motor at LCP-C101 and lock in the OFF position.
3. Isolate the space heater circuit — this is a commonly missed source.
4. Verify zero energy at the machine terminal box using a calibrated proving unit.
   The proving unit must be proved live-dead-live against a known source.

### 2.2 Process Isolation

1. Close and lock the suction isolation valve XV-C101-01.
2. Close and lock the discharge isolation valve XV-C101-02.
3. Insert spades at both flanges per drawing PID-C101-03. **Double block and bleed
   alone is not sufficient for intrusive work on this machine.**
4. Open the bleed to atmosphere and confirm zero pressure at the local gauge.

### 2.3 Seal Gas and Utilities

1. Isolate the seal gas supply at XV-C101-07 and lock.
2. Isolate instrument air at the local filter regulator.
3. Isolate the nitrogen purge connection.

### 2.4 Stored Energy

1. Confirm the rotor has come to a complete stop. Do not rely on the panel
   indication alone — confirm visually at the coupling guard.
2. Depressurise the lube oil accumulator and confirm zero at the local gauge.

## 3. Gas Test Requirements

A gas test must be performed by an authorised gas tester before entry or hot work:

- Oxygen: 19.5% – 23.5%
- LEL: 0% (no detectable flammable gas)
- H₂S: < 5 ppm

**Gas testing must be repeated at intervals not exceeding 4 hours** while work is
in progress, and immediately after any break in work exceeding 30 minutes.

## 4. Permit Requirements

An intrusive maintenance activity on C-101 requires:

1. A **General Work Permit** raised and authorised by the Area Authority.
2. A **Confined Space Entry Permit** if entry into the casing is required.
3. A **Hot Work Permit** if any welding, grinding, or cutting is planned.
4. Isolation certificate signed by the Isolating Authority and countersigned by
   the Performing Authority.

## 5. Verification and Clearance

The Performing Authority must personally verify every isolation point listed in
Section 2 before signing acceptance. Verification by report is not acceptable.

On completion, isolations may only be removed under the authority of the
Isolating Authority, after confirming all personnel and tools are clear.
