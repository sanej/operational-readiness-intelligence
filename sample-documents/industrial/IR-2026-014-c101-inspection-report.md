# IR-2026-014 Statutory Inspection Report — Compressor C-101

**Inspection date:** 2026-02-11 | **Inspector:** A. Vaughn, Asset Integrity
**Next statutory inspection due:** 2027-02-11

> Synthetic document created for demonstration purposes. Fictional site and asset.

## 1. Scope of Inspection

External visual inspection, vibration survey, lube oil analysis, and dry gas seal
performance review of Compressor C-101 in accordance with the site written scheme
of examination.

Internal casing inspection was **not** performed during this examination — the
machine was not opened. Internal inspection remains outstanding and is scheduled
for the next planned shutdown.

## 2. Findings

### F-2026-014-01 — Dry gas seal secondary vent flow elevated (OPEN)

**Severity:** High
**Status:** OPEN

Secondary vent flow on the non-drive-end dry gas seal measured 21 Nm³/h against an
OEM alarm threshold of 15 Nm³/h. The trend shows a steady rise from 9 Nm³/h over
the preceding eight months, consistent with progressive primary seal face
degradation.

This finding is the subject of corrective action **CA-2026-031**, which remains
open at the time of this report.

**Recommendation:** Replace the non-drive-end dry gas seal cartridge at the next
opportunity. Do not return the machine to extended service after any shutdown
without addressing this finding.

### F-2026-014-02 — Baseplate grout deterioration (OPEN)

**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** OPEN

Localised cracking and spalling of the epoxy grout observed at the north-east
holding-down bolt location, approximately 40 mm × 120 mm. Bolt torque was checked
and found within tolerance.

**Recommendation:** Repair grout at the next planned shutdown. Monitor at each
subsequent routine inspection.

### F-2026-014-03 — Vibration monitoring channel bypassed (CLOSED)

**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** CLOSED — verified 2026-02-14

Radial vibration channel 2Y was found bypassed in the monitoring system with no
associated override record. The bypass was removed and the channel returned to
service on 2026-02-14. Verified by B. Iwu, Instrument Technician.
