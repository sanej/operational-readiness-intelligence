# ENV-REQ-07 Environmental Requirements — Venting and Emissions During Maintenance

**Revision:** Rev 3 | **Effective:** 2025-04-01
**Applies to:** All maintenance activities involving depressurisation or venting

> Synthetic document created for demonstration purposes. Fictional site and
> fictional permit conditions. Not derived from any real regulatory instrument.

## 1. Scope

This document sets out the environmental requirements that apply when process gas
is vented or displaced during maintenance activities at Northfield Terminal,
including compressor depressurisation.

## 2. Permit Conditions

Northfield Terminal operates under site environmental permit NT-EP-2019-04. The
following conditions apply to maintenance venting:

### 2.1 Notification

Any planned venting event with an estimated release exceeding **500 kg CH₄** must
be notified to the regulator **not less than 14 days in advance**, using the
prescribed notification form.

Unplanned releases exceeding 500 kg must be reported within 24 hours of discovery.

### 2.2 Quantification

All venting events must be quantified and recorded in the site emissions register
within 5 working days. Quantification method must follow the site emissions
calculation procedure.

### 2.3 Minimisation

Venting to atmosphere is permitted only where recovery to flare or to the fuel gas
system is not reasonably practicable. Where venting is unavoidable, the operator
must demonstrate that inventory has been minimised by depressurising to the lowest
practicable pressure through recovery routes first.

For compressor maintenance, this means the machine must be recycled and
depressurised to the fuel gas system to the maximum practicable extent before any
atmospheric vent is opened.

### 2.4 Weather Restrictions

Atmospheric venting must not be carried out when:

- Wind speed is below 2 m/s (insufficient dispersion), or
- Wind direction places the site boundary within the 100 m downwind sector, or
- A temperature inversion has been forecast or observed.

## 3. Records to be Retained

| Record | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Venting notification and regulator acknowledgement | 6 years |
| Quantification calculation and supporting data | 6 years |
| Weather observations at time of venting | 2 years |
| Emissions register entry | Permanent |

## 4. Responsibilities

The Area Authority is responsible for confirming that all conditions in Section 2
are satisfied before authorising a permit that involves venting. Confirmation must
be recorded on the permit.

Environmental Compliance must be consulted where the estimated release exceeds
500 kg CH₄ or where any doubt exists about the applicability of a condition.
