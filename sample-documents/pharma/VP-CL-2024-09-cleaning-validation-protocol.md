# VP-CL-2024-09 Cleaning Validation Protocol — Line 2 Granulation

**Revision:** Rev 1 | **Effective:** 2024-09-30
**Equipment:** GRN-2100 High-Shear Granulator, Line 2
**Validation status:** Validated — report VR-CL-2024-09 approved 2024-12-18

> Synthetic document created for demonstration purposes. Fictional site,
> equipment, and data.

## 1. Purpose

To demonstrate that the cleaning procedure for GRN-2100 reproducibly reduces
product residue, cleaning agent residue, and bioburden to below the established
acceptance criteria.

## 2. Validation Approach

Three consecutive successful cleaning cycles following the worst-case product
campaign, evaluated against the acceptance criteria in Section 4.

Worst-case product determined to be **PX-200** on the basis of lowest solubility
and highest toxicity ratio among products manufactured on Line 2.

## 3. Scope of Qualification

| Element | Covered by this protocol |
| --- | --- |
| Product-to-product changeover cleaning | Yes |
| Clean hold time up to 72 hours | Yes |
| Clean hold time beyond 72 hours | **No — see Section 7** |
| Dirty hold time up to 24 hours | Yes |
| Manual cleaning of dismantled parts | Yes |
| Cleaning after dedicated-equipment campaigns | No — see SOP-CL-011 |

## 4. Acceptance Criteria

| Test | Criterion | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visual inspection | No visible residue | Direct visual, defined lighting |
| Final rinse conductivity | ≤ 2.5 µS/cm | AM-CON-01 |
| Final rinse TOC | ≤ 500 ppb | AM-TOC-03 |
| Swab — active residue | ≤ 10 µg per location | AM-HPLC-12 |
| Swab — bioburden | ≤ 25 CFU per location | AM-MICRO-02 |

## 5. Results Summary

Three cycles executed 2024-11-04, 2024-11-19, and 2024-12-03. All acceptance
criteria met in all three cycles. Report VR-CL-2024-09 approved 2024-12-18.

Maximum observed values across all cycles and locations:

| Test | Maximum observed | Criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Final rinse conductivity | 1.8 µS/cm | ≤ 2.5 µS/cm |
| Final rinse TOC | 310 ppb | ≤ 500 ppb |
| Swab — active residue | 4.2 µg | ≤ 10 µg |
| Swab — bioburden | 11 CFU | ≤ 25 CFU |

## 6. Revalidation

Revalidation is required:

- Every 3 years from the report approval date — **next due 2027-12-18**
- On any change to the cleaning agent, wash parameters, or acceptance criteria
- On introduction of a new product with a worse solubility or toxicity profile
  than PX-200

## 7. Limitations of This Validation

**Clean hold time beyond 72 hours is not covered by this protocol.** The three
validation cycles held cleaned equipment for 18, 24, and 71 hours respectively.
No cycle held equipment beyond 72 hours, and no bioburden data exists for longer
hold periods.

Any extension of the clean hold time beyond 72 hours requires a supporting
hold-time study and a protocol addendum, neither of which forms part of this
approved protocol.

> **Note for reviewers of this demonstration corpus:** SOP-CL-004 Rev 4 cites
> "VP-CL-2024-09 Addendum 1" as the basis for a 120-hour hold time. No such
> addendum exists in this corpus. This is a deliberate missing-evidence case.
