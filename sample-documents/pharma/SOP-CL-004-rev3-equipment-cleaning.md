# SOP-CL-004 Equipment Cleaning and Changeover — Line 2 Granulation

**Revision:** Rev 3 | **Effective:** 2025-08-12 | **Supersedes:** Rev 2 (2023-02-20)
**Equipment:** GRN-2100 High-Shear Granulator, Line 2
**Approved by:** QA Manager, 2025-08-05

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This procedure defines the cleaning and changeover process for the GRN-2100
high-shear granulator on Line 2 between product campaigns.

It applies to product-to-product changeover. Cleaning after a campaign involving a
dedicated-equipment product is out of scope and governed by SOP-CL-011.

## 2. Change Summary for Rev 3

Rev 3 supersedes Rev 2 in full, issued under change control CC-2025-044:

- Final rinse conductivity limit tightened from ≤ 5.0 µS/cm to **≤ 2.5 µS/cm**.
- Swab sampling locations increased from 4 to **6**, adding the impeller shaft seal
  and the discharge chute weld seam.
- Maximum clean hold time reduced from 96 hours to **72 hours**.

## 3. Cleaning Procedure

### 3.1 Dismantling

1. Confirm the batch is complete and the equipment log is closed out.
2. Remove the impeller and chopper assemblies.
3. Remove the discharge chute and the product contact gaskets.
4. Transfer removable parts to the parts wash station.

### 3.2 Wash Cycle

| Step | Medium | Temperature | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-rinse | Purified water | Ambient | 5 min |
| Detergent wash | 2% alkaline detergent (DET-A2) | 60 ± 5 °C | 15 min |
| Intermediate rinse | Purified water | Ambient | 5 min |
| Final rinse | Water for Injection | Ambient | 10 min |

### 3.3 Acceptance Criteria

The following must all be satisfied before the equipment may be released for the
next product:

| Test | Acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- |
| Visual inspection | No visible residue under defined lighting |
| Final rinse conductivity | ≤ 2.5 µS/cm |
| Final rinse TOC | ≤ 500 ppb |
| Swab — active residue | ≤ 10 µg per swab location |
| Swab — bioburden | ≤ 25 CFU per swab location |

### 3.4 Swab Sampling Locations

Six locations must be sampled at every changeover:

1. Bowl wall, mid-height
2. Bowl base, adjacent to the discharge port
3. Impeller blade root
4. Chopper housing interior
5. Impeller shaft seal **(added Rev 3)**
6. Discharge chute weld seam **(added Rev 3)**

## 4. Clean Hold Time

Cleaned equipment may be held for a maximum of **72 hours** before use. Beyond
72 hours, the equipment must be re-cleaned from Section 3.1.

Clean hold time is recorded on the equipment status label and in the equipment log.

## 5. Documentation

The completed cleaning record must include:

- Operator identification and date/time of each stage
- All acceptance-criteria results with analyst identification
- Second-person verification signature for the visual inspection
- Equipment status label reference

## 6. Release for Next Product

Equipment may only be released for the next product after the cleaning record is
reviewed and signed by QA. Release is a QA decision and may not be made by
manufacturing personnel.
