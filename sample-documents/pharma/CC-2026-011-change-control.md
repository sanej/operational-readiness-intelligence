## 1. Proposed Change

Extend the maximum clean hold time for GRN-2100 from 72 hours to 120 hours, and
reduce swab sampling locations from 6 to 4, by issuing SOP-CL-004 Rev 4.

## 2. Justification

Twelve consecutive changeovers returned no detectable active residue at the
impeller shaft seal and discharge chute weld seam locations. A hold-time study was
proposed to support the extension to 120 hours.
